## Candidate slate (ranked by signal × fit with the cutaway build chain)
1. **Backyard hole → fallout shelter.** Strongest proven format plus fallout search interest. The chain covers air, radiation shielding by depth, water, waste, and the 2-week stay.
2. **Ordinary house → blackout-proof house.** Grid-stress trend. The chain covers fridge, heat/cold, water pump, batteries, solar, and comms.
3. **Backpack → 72-hour go bag.** TikTok go-bag trend. The cutaway shows the bag interior; the chain is water → filter → food → light → warmth → documents.
4. **Garage → solar-storm-ready garage.** EMP/solar curiosity. The chain covers electronics, Faraday cage, fuel, and manual tools. Keep claims sober.
5. **Closet → 1-year pantry.** Shelf-life angle. The chain covers spoilage, rodents, moisture, rotation, and oxygen absorbers.
6. **Rain barrel → 2-week water supply.** Verify the FEMA/Ready.gov per-person figure. The chain covers algae, mosquitoes, filtering, and storage.
7. **Small apartment → renter's safe room.** Fits the "everyday prepper" shift and suits a non-homeowner audience.
8. **$500 hole vs $25M bunker.** A luxury-bunker curiosity format, possibly a two-part or comparison variant.
9. **Basement → root cellar (no-power fridge).** Evergreen and low-fear.
10. **Car → bug-out vehicle.** Evergreen, with a mobility angle.

## Topic guardrails
- Facts and quantities must come from FEMA/Ready.gov, CDC, NOAA SWPC, or similar primary sources, cited in each project's script notes.
- No fearmongering about specific dated catastrophes, no conspiracy framing, no weapons or tactics content, no medical dosing advice.
- Tone: practical, curious and reassuring. Every Short ends with the viewer feeling more capable, not more afraid.
- Construction content is illustrative. Add an on-screen or description note: "not engineering advice — check local codes."
