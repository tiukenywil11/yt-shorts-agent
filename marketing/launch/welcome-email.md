# Small Change Lab: welcome email

Draft. Set it up in Brevo as an automation that sends when a contact joins the newsletter list, after double opt-in.

**Subject options**
1. Your free tracker, and the number behind it (recommended)
2. It probably takes longer than 21 days
3. One small thing to try today

**Preview text:** The tracker link, one cited fact, and one thing to try today.

---

Hi, and thanks for confirming.

Here's the free 30-day habit tracker:
https://smallchangelab.kenywil.com/tracker.html?ref=email

No account needed. Your ticks are saved only in the browser and device you use, so stick to one, or use the backup button.

**One fact, with its limits.** You've probably heard that a habit takes 21 days. In one study of 96 students, the median time for a new habit to feel automatic was 66 days. Individual results ranged from 18 to 254 days, and the top of that range was a projection, not something the researchers watched happen.

So a missed week of "21 days" doesn't mean it failed. You may just be early.

**Try it today:** pick one habit small enough to do on your worst day, and tick day 1.

What's the habit? Hit reply and tell me. I read these, and it helps me choose what to research next.

From here on, you'll hear from me only occasionally: new Shorts, the cited fact behind each one, and one thing to try. It's short and free.

Small Change Lab

*Source: Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle (2010), European Journal of Social Psychology, doi:10.1002/ejsp.674. General information, not medical advice.*

## Before you turn it on

- [ ] Add a real postal address to the email footer. US law (CAN-SPAM) requires one on commercial email. A PO box or registered mailbox works if you'd rather not use your home address.
- [ ] Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link.
- [ ] Make sure replies reach an inbox you read, because the email asks for them and `privacy.html` tells people to reply to request deletion.
- [ ] Send a test to yourself, then click the tracker link.
