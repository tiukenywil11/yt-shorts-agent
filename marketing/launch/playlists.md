# Small Change Lab: playlists

Create 8 playlists, one per series and pillar. A video goes in its pillar playlist and, if it belongs to one, its series playlist. Mapping is from each `projects/NN/idea.md`. Series with no videos yet: create the playlist after the first episode so it is not empty at launch (or add the trailer as a placeholder). Set the trailer as the channel trailer for non-subscribers, not in a playlist.

## Series

### 1. 7 Fixes Later
Description: An ordinary morning, paycheck or evening gets 7 quick fixes. One cited study, seven small things to try. Educational only, not financial or medical advice.
Videos: none yet (no `projects/` episode is in this series).

### 2. Habit Myths
Description: One habit myth, what the research actually says, and the real habit to try. Each Short names the study and its limits.
Videos:
- `03-motivation-vs-discipline` (Mind): "Do You Need Motivation to Start?"
- `06-snooze-button` (Body): "Is Hitting Snooze Really That Bad?"

### 3. The Numbers
Description: One stat brought to life with a worked, labelled-illustrative number. Small amounts over time, shown clearly. Not financial advice.
Videos: none yet.

### 4. 30-Day Log
Description: My own 30-day habit experiments, with my numbers and no hype. Not a study and not typical results.
Videos: none yet.

## Pillars

### Money
Description: Small money habits backed by cited research: saving, spending and setting up simple defaults. General education, not financial advice.
Videos:
- `04-coin-jar-savings`: "Does a Visible Savings Goal Help?"
- `05-pay-yourself-first`: "Does Automatic Saving Beat Willpower?"

### Mind
Description: Focus, motivation and habit-forming, explained by studies. One small change to try today.
Videos:
- `01-focus-block`: "Every Switch Costs You Time"
- `03-motivation-vs-discipline`: "Do You Need Motivation to Start?" (also in Habit Myths)

### Body
Description: Sleep and morning routines, explained by studies. General information, not medical advice.
Videos:
- `02-night-wind-down`: "Could Your Bedtime Screen Keep You Up?"
- `06-snooze-button`: "Is Hitting Snooze Really That Bad?" (also in Habit Myths)

### Ready
Description: Small steps to be prepared, one item at a time. General guidance only; follow your local emergency-management advice.
Videos:
- `07-emergency-kit-payday`: "Build an Emergency Kit, One Item per Payday"
