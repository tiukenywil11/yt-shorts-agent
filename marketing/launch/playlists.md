# Small Change Lab: playlists

Create 8 playlists, one per series and pillar. A video goes in its pillar playlist and, if it belongs to one, its series playlist. Mapping is from each `projects/NN/idea.md`. Series with no videos yet: create the playlist after the first episode so it is not empty.

Which version to add: use the version that is public (see `creative/publishing-log.csv`). For episodes 01 and 02, the v1 is public now and the v2 is scheduled. Swap in the v2 when it goes live, so a playlist never shows the same topic twice. Keep the v1 public.

## Series

### 1. 7 Fixes Later
Description: An ordinary morning, paycheck or evening gets 7 quick fixes. One cited study, seven small things to try. Educational only, not financial or medical advice.
Videos: none yet.

### 2. Habit Myths
Description: One habit myth, what the research actually says, and the real habit to try. Each Short names the study and its limits.
Videos:
- `03-motivation-vs-discipline-v2` (Mind): "Do You Need Motivation to Start?"
- `06-snooze-button-v2` (Body): "Is Hitting Snooze Really That Bad?"
- `08-habit-21-days` (Mind): "21 Days to Form a Habit?"

### 3. The Numbers
Description: One stat brought to life with a worked, labelled-illustrative number. Small amounts over time, shown clearly. Not financial advice.
Videos: none yet.

### 4. 30-Day Log
Description: My own 30-day habit experiments, with my numbers and no hype. Not a study and not typical results.
Videos: none yet.
