## Disclaimers (post on About and on the website footer)

**Not financial advice.** Small Change Lab is educational and general. We do not recommend specific stocks, funds, accounts or products for your situation, and we promise no returns or income. Talk to a licensed professional before you make financial decisions.

**Not medical advice.** Sleep, exercise and health content is general information, not diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor or other qualified clinician about your own health.

**Emergency preparedness.** Ready videos are general. Follow your local emergency-management guidance, which varies by place and household.

**Research limits.** We name the study and its limits (sample size, who was studied, what it could not show). One study is not proof. Results vary from person to person.

**Creator results.** In the 30-Day Log series, numbers are the creator's own, labelled "my numbers, not a study". They are not typical results.

**Illustrative numbers.** Worked examples (for example, compound-interest figures) are labelled illustrative and are not predictions.

## Sources policy

1. Every Short with a factual claim cites its source on screen (study card) and in the description.
2. We prefer peer-reviewed studies and government health and finance data (for example CDC, NIH, FEMA, CFPB) over blogs or social posts.
3. Cards state the study, year, sample size when known, and the limit. We use hedged language ("in one study", "may") unless the evidence is strong.
4. Full citations live in each video's description. If a source turns out to be wrong or retracted, we correct it with a pinned comment and update the description.
