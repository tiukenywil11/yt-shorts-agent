## 2. Description

Only 53% of households in one survey had three days of water, so try one emergency-kit item per payday, water first. In an online survey of 572 adults (August 2014), 96% met the 3-day food guidance but 53% met the 3-day water guidance. Try it today: add one gallon of water to your shopping list.
Source: Hiatt, E., Belliard, C., Lloyd Call, M. A., Jefferies, L. K., Kener, M., Eggett, D. L., & Richards, R. (2021 online; 2022 issue). Household Food and Water Emergency Preparedness Practices Across the United States. Disaster Medicine and Public Health Preparedness, 16(3), 1029-1037. doi:10.1017/dmp.2020.480
FOLLOW LOCAL GUIDANCE. One self-reported survey, so results may differ for you. The three steps are a practical way to start and were not tested in the study. Ready.gov lists one gallon of water per person per day for at least three days: https://www.ready.gov/kit and https://www.ready.gov/sites/default/files/documents/files/checklist3.pdf
