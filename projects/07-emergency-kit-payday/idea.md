# emergency-kit-payday

- Created: 2026-11-08
- Channel: Small Change Lab
- Pillar / series: Ready / standard (problem -> fix, 3 steps)
- Working title: "Build an Emergency Kit, One Item per Payday"
- Topic: #8 in `creative/trending-habit-topics-2026-11.xlsx` (emergency kit, one item per payday, trend score 15.36), tied to slate #14. Chosen for pillar balance: episodes 1-6 were Mind, Body, Mind, Money, Money, Body; Ready (target ~20%) has none yet. Money tie-in (payday cadence) without financial advice.
- Central idea: a full emergency kit feels too big to start, so people stall. A survey found nearly all households met FEMA's 3-day food guidance but only about half met it for water. Buy one item per payday, water first.
- Audience: students and early-career workers who mean to prepare but haven't started.
- Hook (outcome+number, sentence 1 <= 12 words): "In one survey, only 53% of households stored three days of water." Batch log: outcome, claim, myth, claim, outcome, myth, outcome.
- Payoff: three steps (water, one shelf-stable food on the next list, one labeled bin), then "Try it today: add one gallon of water to your shopping list."
- Visual idea: Inch beside an empty bin and a long checklist; Coral cross on "too big". Cite card with 96% vs 53%. Three step slates. Ready tint, FOLLOW LOCAL GUIDANCE tag from the cite card on. Reuse Paper Prepper-style props only as generic items (water jug, can, bin); no Tuck, no READY stamp.
- Ending: no trailing scene; ends ~0.1 s after voiceover.
