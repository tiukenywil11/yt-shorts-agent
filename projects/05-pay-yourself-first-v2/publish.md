## 2. Description

37% of new hires at one company joined their savings plan when they had to opt in; 86% did when they were signed up unless they opted out. Try it today: schedule one tiny transfer for your next payday.
Source: Madrian, B. C., & Shea, D. F. (2001). The power of suggestion: Inertia in 401(k) participation and savings behavior. The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 116(4), 1149-1187. doi:10.1162/003355301753265543. The 37% and 86% figures are from the NBER Working Paper 7682 version (3-15 months of tenure); the published article was not read.
NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE. This was one company's plan, so results may differ for you. The payday transfer is a practical way to try the idea and was not tested in the study.
