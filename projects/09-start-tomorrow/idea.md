## Source
Steel, P. (2007). The nature of procrastination: a meta-analytic and theoretical review of quintessential self-regulatory failure. Psychological Bulletin, 133(1), 65-94, doi:10.1037/0033-2909.133.1.65. Abstract read 2026-11-09 via University of Calgary repository post-print (not copy of record): meta-analysis of 691 correlations; strong, consistent predictors: task aversiveness, task delay, self-efficacy, impulsiveness, conscientiousness (and facets); weak: neuroticism, rebelliousness, sensation seeking.
