## 2. Description

In one survey of 572 adults, 96% had three days of food but only 53% had three days of water. Start with a gallon of water per person per day. Guidance: https://www.ready.gov/water
Source: Hiatt, E., Belliard, C., Lloyd Call, M. A., Jefferies, L. K., Kener, M., Eggett, D. L., & Richards, R. (2021 online; Vol. 16 issue 3, 2022). Household Food and Water Emergency Preparedness Practices Across the United States. Disaster Medicine and Public Health Preparedness, 16(3), 1029-1037. doi:10.1017/dmp.2020.480
FOLLOW LOCAL GUIDANCE. The survey was administered in 2014 and was self-reported, so results may differ for you. The steps are a practical way to try the idea and were not tested in the study.
