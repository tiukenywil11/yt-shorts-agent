## 2. Description

Switching tasks has a time cost. Try one task, a 20-minute timer you can see, your phone face down, and a "later" list.
Source: Rubinstein, J. S., Meyer, D. E., & Evans, J. E. (2001). Executive control of cognitive processes in task switching. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance, 27(4), 763–797. doi:10.1037/0096-1523.27.4.763
Caveat: in one lab study with simple tasks. Real work varies, and some jobs need quick switching.
