## 2. Description

Same money, two accounts instead of one: in one study, people saved more. Split a goal's savings in two and keep a picture of the goal in sight.
Source: Soman, D., & Cheema, A. (2011). Earmarking and partitioning: Increasing saving by low-income households. Journal of Marketing Research, 48(SPL), S14-S22. doi:10.1509/jmkr.48.spl.s14
NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE. One study of low-income consumers, so results may differ for you. The steps are a practical way to try the idea and were not tested together in the study.
