## 2. Description

Disciplined people may not be using more willpower. In one set of studies, better self-control was linked with relying more on habits and less effortful resisting. Try one cue and two minutes today.
Source: Galla, B. M., & Duckworth, A. L. (2015). More than resisting temptation: Beneficial habits mediate the relationship between self-control and positive life outcomes. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 109(3), 508-525. doi:10.1037/pspp0000026 (PMID 25643222)
Limits: the results are linked, not proven, and mostly self-reported. The cue and two-minute steps are a practical way to try the idea and were not tested in the study. Habits take time to form.
