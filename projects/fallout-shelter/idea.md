# fallout-shelter

- Created: 2026-11-06
- Channel: Paper Prepper (working title)
- Working title: "A Hole, 9 Fixes Later"
- Topic: a plain backyard hole becomes a two-week fallout shelter through 9 problem → fix beats (slate #1, `creative/topic-research.md`).
- Central idea: shelter from fallout is mostly simple physics — mass between you and the dust, air, water, and time. Time is on your side.
- Audience: US/English 18–40, curious about everyday preparedness, not hardcore survivalists.
- Hook: "This hole is nine problems away from a fallout shelter."
- Twist (45–55 s): fallout loses strength fast (7-10 rule).
- Payoff: night pull-back of the finished, lit shelter with Tuck inside; READY stamp.
- Tone: practical, curious, lightly witty. No blasts, mushroom clouds, or casualties.
- End-card note: "Illustration only — not engineering advice. Check local codes and official guidance at ready.gov."

## Fact sources (verify before final)
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Fallout is radioactive dust/particles that settle from the air | Ready.gov "Nuclear Explosion"; CDC "Radiation Emergencies: Fallout" |
| ~3 ft of packed earth gives large protection (~1000× reduction; halving thickness of earth ≈ 3.6 in) | C. Kearny, *Nuclear War Survival Skills*, Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL-5037); FEMA civil-defense shielding guidance |
| Turns in an entryway reduce radiation reaching the room | Kearny, NWSS ch. 5 (shelter entrances) |
| Sealed shelters need forced ventilation | Kearny, NWSS ch. 7 (ventilation, KAP pump) |
| Water: 1 gallon per person per day | Ready.gov "Water" / "Build a Kit" |
| Avoid open flames in a closed space; prefer battery lights | Ready.gov "Build a Kit" (flashlight, batteries); CDC indoor air / CO guidance |
| Hand-crank or battery radio for official info | Ready.gov "Build a Kit" |
| "Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned" | Ready.gov / CDC radiation emergency messaging |
| 7-10 rule: 7 h → ~1/10 dose rate; 49 h → ~1/100 | FEMA/civil-defense 7-10 rule; Kearny NWSS ch. 1 |
| Plan for up to ~2 weeks of sheltering | Kearny NWSS; FEMA historical shelter-stay guidance (official current advice: at least 24 h unless told otherwise — keep "ready for two weeks" as a capacity claim, not an instruction) |
