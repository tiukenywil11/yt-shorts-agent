## Description
```
This hole is nine problems away from a fallout shelter. Every fix causes the next problem. Can Tuck the mole get it ready?

From roof and packed dirt to air, water, light and radio, here are 9 simple ideas behind a basic fallout shelter. The twist at the end: fallout fades fast. After 7 hours it is about 10x weaker, after 2 days about 100x weaker.

The 9 fixes:
1. Roof, to keep fallout dust out
2. 3 feet of packed dirt for shielding
3. Timber beams for support
4. A bent entrance (baffle entry)
5. Hand-cranked fan and filtered vent
6. Water: 1 gallon per person per day
7. Lidded bucket toilet
8. Battery lanterns, not candles
9. Hand-crank radio: get inside, stay inside, stay tuned

Official guidance: https://www.ready.gov/nuclear-explosion

Illustration only. Not engineering advice. Check local codes and official guidance at ready.gov.

Paper Prepper: one problem and one fix at a time. Subscribe for the next build.

Voiceover is AI-generated.
```
