## 2. Description

Forget 21 days: one study estimated habits take anywhere from 18 to 254 days, so day 22 feeling hard does not mean you are failing. Try it today: pick one habit and mark day one.
Source: Lally, P., van Jaarsveld, C. H. M., Potts, H. W. W., & Wardle, J. (2010). European Journal of Social Psychology, 40(6), 998-1009. doi:10.1002/ejsp.674
Limits: 96 volunteers, 12 weeks, self-reported automaticity; 82 analysed, and the model fit was good for only 39. The 18 to 254 range is a model estimate, 21 days was not tested, and the steps are a practical way to try the idea, not something the study tested.
