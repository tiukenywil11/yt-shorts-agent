## 2. Description

One quick text is really two task switches: away, then back. In one 2001 lab study, people were slower when they switched tasks. Try one 20-minute focus block today.
Source: Rubinstein, J. S., Meyer, D. E., & Evans, J. E. (2001). Executive control of cognitive processes in task switching. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance, 27(4), 763-797. doi:10.1037/0096-1523.27.4.763
Caveat: "two" is a plain-language framing (away and back), not a count measured in the study. The lab tasks were simple, and real work varies. The 20-minute block is a practical way to try the idea and was not tested in the study.
