## 2. Description

A visible savings goal may help you save more. In one study of low-income consumers, a visual reminder of the goal raised the savings rate, and splitting money into two parts helped too. Try it today: name one goal and keep it where you can see it.
Source: Soman, D., & Cheema, A. (2011). Earmarking and partitioning: Increasing saving by low-income households. Journal of Marketing Research, 48(SPL), S14-S22. doi:10.1509/jmkr.48.spl.s14
NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE. This was one study of low-income consumers, so results may differ for you. The three steps are a practical way to try the idea and were not tested together in the study.
