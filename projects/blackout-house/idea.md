# blackout-house

- Created: 2026-11-06
- Channel: Paper Prepper (working title)
- Working title: "A House, 7 Fixes Later"
- Topic: an ordinary house survives a multi-day blackout through 7 problem -> fix beats (slate #2, `creative/topic-research.md`; grid-stress / home-battery interest). Not covered by `fallout-shelter`.
- Central idea: blackout prep is mostly small, boring habits: keep cold things closed, light safely, charge, store water, shrink your space, vent fuel outdoors, stay informed.
- Audience: US/English 18-40, curious about everyday preparedness, not hardcore survivalists. Renters and homeowners alike.
- Hook: "This ordinary house is seven problems away from surviving a blackout."
- Payoff (final 10 s): seven fixes, ordinary house ready for the dark; closer "Ready beats lucky."
- Sources to cite in script notes: Ready.gov power outage guidance (water 1 gal/person/day, lanterns not candles); FDA/USDA food safety in power outages (fridge ~4 h, full freezer ~48 h); CDC/CPSC generator safety (outdoors, >=20 ft from home, CO alarm).
- Guardrails: no fearmongering, no dated-catastrophe claims, no medical dosing. End-card note: "not safety advice — follow local guidance."
