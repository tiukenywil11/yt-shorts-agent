Scope: 15 starter Shorts (Money 6, Mind 4, Body 3, Ready 2) for the problem→fix format with one on-screen cite card.
Evidence status: **no trend data pulled** (no Google Trends, YouTube autocomplete or analytics). Every source below is a **source type to find and verify**, not a citation. Before scripting, put the exact study/report (authors, year, n, limit, link) in `projects/<id>/idea.md`. If no credible source supports the claim, drop or reframe the topic.
