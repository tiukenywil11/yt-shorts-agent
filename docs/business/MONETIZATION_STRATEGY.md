# Monetization & Growth Strategy

Decided 2026-11-06. Paper Prepper (home preparedness) was the proof of concept for the engine. The business channel is a **faceless animated "science of small habits" channel with money as its main pillar**, built in public: the creator practices the habits too.

> Thresholds, rates and policies are as of mid-2026. Check YouTube Studio → Earn and the current YouTube policy pages before relying on them.

What decided it: the creator wants to practice these habits (authenticity, and it helps with burnout), the long-form science angle is evergreen, and money videos keep earning power high.

## Formats
- **Shorts (discovery):** one small habit, problem→fix, 30–50 s. Show the study or stat on screen and end on "try it today."
- **Long-form (income):** 8–12 minute "the science behind ___" explainers grown from winning Shorts. Each is cut back into 2–3 Shorts that point to it with the Related Video link and pinned comment. Needs a 16:9 engine variant, scoped separately.
- **Build in public:** a monthly "I tried this habit for 30 days" update with your own numbers (savings rate, sleep log). This is the authenticity anchor. Use your own voice on long-form at least sometimes.

## Series
1. **7 Fixes Later:** an ordinary morning, paycheck or evening gets 7 small fixes.
2. **Habit Myths:** one myth (for example "21 days to form a habit"), what the research actually says, the real habit.
3. **The Numbers:** one stat brought to life (compound interest, sleep debt, the cost of a daily coffee over 10 years).
4. **30-Day Log:** the creator's own experiment, with results.

## Funnel & product ladder
Short → profile link / pinned comment → **free habit tracker** (email signup) → paid planner → course.
1. Free: a 1-page 30-day habit tracker (printable).
2. $9–19: "Small Habits Planner" (habits + budget + savings tracker), printable.
3. $19–29: Notion or Google Sheets life/money dashboard.
4. Later, $49+: a "30-Day Reset" email course (one habit per pillar).
5. Later: mascot merch (stickers, fridge magnets with habit rules).

Tools: Gumroad, Stan Store or Payhip; Kit (ConvertKit) or Beehiiv for email. Product art reuses the channel SVG assets. Shorts descriptions have limited clickable links, so use channel profile links and pinned comments.

## Posting
- Months 0–3: Shorts 4–5 a week, rotating pillars. From month 2–3: long-form every 1–2 weeks. Monthly 30-Day Log.
- Hook in 1–2 s, target 70%+ viewed vs swiped, loopable ending, searchable title, 3–5 hashtags, pinned comment with the tracker link.
- Cite sources in each project's `idea.md` (peer-reviewed studies and government health/finance data first).
- Produce in batches with a 2-week buffer. Review analytics every 2 weeks.
- Calendar: New Year resets (huge for habits), tax season, back-to-school, Black Friday spending, National Preparedness Month (September, Ready pillar).

## Risks & guardrails
- **Crowded "motivation" space:** stand out with the paper-cutout problem→fix format, a cited study on screen, and real personal results. No generic motivational quotes.
- **Inauthentic content (2025 policy):** every video needs specific sourced facts and a distinct build. Never publish the same script with nouns swapped. Build-in-public videos and your own voice help.
- **Pop-science:** don't overstate research. Name the study and its limits.
- **Money and health scrutiny:** no stock picks, income promises, medical or supplement claims.
- **Burnout:** no revenue pressure for the first 3 months. If it starts to feel like work, slow down.

## Branding
- New name and mascot (not Tuck). Check the handle, domain and USPTO trademark before committing.
- Position: "small habits, explained by science, practiced in public."
- Assets: avatar, banner, 30 s trailer, About page with disclaimers and a sources policy, a playlist per series and pillar, and a website with an email list from day 1.
