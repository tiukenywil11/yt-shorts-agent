# Animation and MP4 QA Checklist

## Story
- [ ] Hook communicates a clear promise.
- [ ] Story is understandable with audio muted.
- [ ] Payoff resolves the setup.
- [ ] No unsupported factual claims.

## Visual
- [ ] Character appearance is consistent.
- [ ] No broken anatomy, poses, or merged objects.
- [ ] No accidental cropping.
- [ ] Text is readable and within safe margins.
- [ ] Background does not overpower the subject.
- [ ] No unintended static/frozen frames.
- [ ] Motion is smooth and intentional.
- [ ] Transitions do not flash or expose blank frames.

## Audio
- [ ] Narration is intelligible.
- [ ] No clipping or abrupt cuts.
- [ ] Voice and visuals are synchronized.
- [ ] Music and SFX do not mask narration.
- [ ] No accidental silence.

## Technical
- [ ] Correct 9:16 dimensions.
- [ ] Correct frame rate.
- [ ] H.264 video and AAC audio (or explicitly approved alternative).
- [ ] Full file decodes.
- [ ] Duration is correct.
- [ ] No black frames or missing scenes.
- [ ] Final output exists and is non-empty.

## Severity
- Critical: prevents publishing or breaks story comprehension.
- Major: obvious defect affecting quality.
- Minor: polish issue that does not block publishing.
